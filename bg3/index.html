How the vocabulary was chosen

The glossary favours Germanic English roots, archaic dialogue, weaponry, creatures, and less familiar meanings. It omits close Catalan/Spanish cognates such as “necromancer”, “infernal”, and “ritual”. Some short words remain because their fantasy meaning is specialised.

Translations explain the meaning in Catalan; they are not claimed to be official D&D terminology. Words can have other meanings: always read the context. The bundled game sentences have been reviewed for meaning and translated into Catalan. New automatic matches may need review.

The app includes Catalan translations for its curated game sentences and practice examples. It does not automatically translate arbitrary new dialogue. Game entries retain their XML content ID for traceability. Your file may also contain books, item descriptions, and interface text, since a localisation XML does not always identify dialogue separately.

To rebuild the standalone app from a locally available XML, run python3 scripts/import_xml.py data/english.xml. The original bytes remain in data/english.xml. See README.md for details.
